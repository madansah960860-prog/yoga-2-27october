What you need

A simple setup at home

  • A laptop, tablet or phone with a camera, placed 6–8 feet away so your whole body is visible
  • A stable internet connection; use headphones if your room echoes
  • Enough floor space to lie down with your arms stretched out
  • A non-slip mat. Optional: two blocks, a strap and a firm blanket (books, a belt and a towel work too)
  • Comfortable clothing that lets your teacher see the shape of your knees and spine

Choosing a session

Which session is right for you?

Choose Professional if you…

  • are new to yoga, or returning after a long break
  • want to check your alignment in foundational poses
  • would like a gentle or chair-based practice adapted to you
  • want regular accountability at an accessible price

Choose Expert if you…

  • have a consistent practice and want to progress safely to arm balances or inversions
  • want structured instruction in pranayama or meditation
  • are a teacher or trainee looking for mentoring on sequencing and cueing
  • prefer a written plan to follow between sessions

Fair policies

Cancellations & refunds

  • Free cancellation or rescheduling up to 24 hours before your session start time.
  • Late cancellations (under 24 hours) aren’t refunded, but you can reschedule once within 30 days.

  • If we cancel or a technical problem on our side stops the session, choose a full refund or a free new session.

Session questions

Good to know

Can I buy more than one session at a time?

Yes. Just mention how many sessions you’d like when you write to us. Each session is charged at the same listed price, and each one follows the same 24-hour cancellation policy.

Can I switch between Professional and Expert sessions?

Of course. You can choose a different session type each time you book; there is no commitment.

Do you offer sessions for couples or small groups?

Our sessions are designed as one-to-one instruction. If you’d like to practice with a partner in the same room, contact us first so we can let you know whether it will work for your goals.

What if my internet connection drops?

Your teacher will wait and reconnect with you. If more than 15 minutes are lost because of a problem on our side, we’ll offer a free make-up session.

Ready to practice together?

Tell us a little about yourself and the times that suit you. We reply within one business day.
