You must be at least 18 years old to book a session. Minors may take part only with the consent and presence of a parent or legal guardian, who accepts these Terms on their behalf.

3. Sessions and pricing

  • Professional Session: $49 USD for a 60-minute live, one-to-one online session.
  • Expert Session: $89 USD for a 75-minute live, one-to-one online session with a senior teacher, including the extras described on our Sessions & Pricing page.

Prices are per session, in US dollars. There are no subscriptions, sign-up fees or automatic renewals. Applicable sales tax, if any, is shown before payment. We may change prices in the future, but a change will never affect a session you have already paid for.

4. Booking and payment

To book, send a request through our contact page. We will confirm an available time and email you a secure payment link from our payment processor. A session is booked once payment is complete. You will not be charged until you accept a confirmed time.

5. Your responsibilities during sessions

  • Tell your teacher about any injuries, medical conditions, pregnancy or recent surgery before the session begins.
  • Practice in a safe, clear space on a non-slip surface, within your own limits. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or discomfort.
  • Consult your doctor before starting if you have any health concerns. You take part voluntarily and accept responsibility for your own wellbeing during practice.
  • Sessions are private. Please don’t record a session unless your teacher agrees; Expert sessions include an official recording on request.

6. Cancellation, rescheduling and refunds

  • More than 24 hours’ notice: cancel for a full refund or reschedule free of charge.
  • Less than 24 hours’ notice: the session fee is not refunded, but you may reschedule once within 30 days.
  • Missed sessions: if you don’t join within 15 minutes of the start time without notice, the session is treated as completed.
  • First-session guarantee: if you aren’t satisfied with your first session with us, email us within 7 days and we’ll refund it in full. No forms or explanations required.
  • If we cancel: if your teacher has to cancel, or a technical problem on our side prevents the session, you can choose a full refund or a free rescheduled session.

Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days of approval. To cancel or request a refund, email hello@asanaatlas.us.
