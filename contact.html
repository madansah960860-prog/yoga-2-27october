Write to us

Ask a question about your practice, request a live session, point out something we could explain better, or make a privacy request. A real person reads every message, and we aim to reply within one business day.

Send a message

Submitting opens your email app with your message ready to send. Prefer to write directly? Email hello@asanaatlas.us. Please don’t include detailed medical records; a short note about injuries or conditions is enough.
