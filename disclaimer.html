Health & Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: October 27, 2026

In short: Asana Atlas teaches yoga as education and movement practice. We are not doctors, physical therapists or mental-health professionals, and nothing on this Site or in our sessions is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Not medical advice

The information on asanaatlas.us, including pose instructions, benefits, sequences, breathing techniques and meditation guidance, is provided for general educational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or another qualified health provider with any questions you have about a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you read on this Site.

Check with your doctor first if you…

  • are pregnant, trying to conceive or have recently given birth;
  • have high or low blood pressure, a heart condition, or a history of stroke;
  • have glaucoma, a detached retina or another eye condition (especially before inversions);
  • have osteoporosis, joint replacements, or back, neck, hip, knee or shoulder problems;
  • have had surgery or an injury recently;
  • live with epilepsy, diabetes, a respiratory condition, or any chronic illness;
  • are managing anxiety, trauma, depression or another mental-health condition, particularly before intensive breathwork.

Practice within your limits

Yoga involves physical movement and carries a risk of injury, as any physical activity does. Move slowly, use props, and never force a pose. Stop immediately and seek help if you feel pain, numbness, dizziness, shortness of breath or chest discomfort. Breathing techniques that involve breath retention or rapid breathing (such as Kapalabhati) are not suitable for everyone; we flag these clearly where they appear.

About the benefits we describe

When we describe benefits of a pose or practice, we mean effects that are commonly reported by practitioners or taught within yoga traditions, or that are explored in published research. Individual experiences vary, and we don’t promise any specific physical, mental or health outcome. Yoga is not a cure or treatment for any disease.

Photographs

Photographs on the Site are illustrative. They show experienced practitioners, sometimes in fuller expressions of a pose than a beginner should attempt. Follow the written instructions and modifications rather than trying to copy a photograph exactly.

Live sessions

Our teachers adapt sessions to the information you share, but they cannot assess or treat medical conditions. By booking, you confirm that you are physically able to take part or have medical clearance to do so, and that you will tell your teacher about relevant conditions. See our Terms.

Emergencies

If you think you may have a medical emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number immediately. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) in the United States.

External links

We sometimes link to external sources for further reading. We don’t control and aren’t responsible for the content of those websites.
