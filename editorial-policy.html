Editorial Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Asana Atlas exists to give people clear, careful and honest information about yoga. This page explains the standards behind every guide on the Site.

How we write

  • Instructions come first. Every pose entry gives step-by-step entry and exit instructions, common modifications, and cautions, so readers can practice sensibly rather than copy a photo.
  • Grounded in established teaching. Our pose and breathing guidance reflects alignment principles widely taught across major yoga lineages, and our history pages draw on classical texts such as the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali, the Bhagavad Gita and the Hatha Yoga Pradipika, along with published scholarship.
  • Plain language. We explain Sanskrit terms the first time we use them and avoid jargon.

How we describe benefits

We describe what a pose or practice is traditionally used for and what practitioners commonly report. We avoid words such as “cure,” “heal” or “treat,” we don’t promise weight loss or specific medical outcomes, and we include safety cautions wherever a practice isn’t suitable for everyone. Read our Health Disclaimer.

Review and updates

Guides are reviewed by an experienced yoga teacher before publication and revisited periodically. When we make a meaningful change, we update the page.

Corrections

If you spot an error, an unclear instruction or a missing caution, please tell us at hello@asanaatlas.us. We review every correction request and fix confirmed errors promptly.

Independence and advertising

Our content is written independently. Advertising, where it appears, is clearly separated from our guides and is never presented as editorial content. Advertisers do not influence what we write, and we do not accept payment to recommend products. If we ever recommend equipment, we will say clearly whether we have any commercial relationship with the brand.

Our own services

We offer paid one-to-one sessions, and we mention them on relevant pages. Our free guides are complete on their own; you never need to buy a session to follow them safely.

Photography

We use real photographs of people practicing yoga, shared by their photographers under open licenses (Creative Commons or public domain) that permit reuse. We don’t use AI-generated images of people. Full credits are listed on our About page.
