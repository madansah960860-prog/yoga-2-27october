Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Asana Atlas (“Asana Atlas,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects information when you visit asanaatlas.us (the “Site”) or book a live session with us. We keep the information we collect to the minimum needed to run the Site and our services.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact and booking details: your name, email address, time zone, preferred session times and the content of messages you send us.
  • Practice information you choose to share: for example, your experience level, goals or a brief note about an injury, so a teacher can plan a safe session. Please share only what is necessary.
  • Payment confirmation: payments are handled by a third-party payment processor. We receive confirmation that a payment was made, but we do not receive or store your full card number.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, referring pages and the date and time of your visit.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: small files stored on your device that help a site function, measure traffic and, where used, show advertising. See our Cookie Policy.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to your questions and arrange, deliver and follow up on sessions you request.
  • To send booking confirmations, payment links, session links and practice plans you have asked for.
  • To operate, maintain, secure and improve the Site and understand which content is useful.
  • To display advertising that helps keep our educational content free to read (where advertising is enabled).
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not send marketing emails unless you have asked to receive them.

3. Advertising and Google

We may show advertising on the Site and may advertise the Site using Google services. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices. To learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Analytics

We may use analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the Site in aggregate. These tools use cookies to collect information such as pages viewed and approximate location. You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting data by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. Third-party services

The Site loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google servers and shares your IP address to download them. Live sessions use a third-party video platform, and payments use a third-party payment processor. Each of these providers processes data under its own privacy policy.

6. How we share information

We share personal information only:

  • with service providers who help us run the Site and our sessions (hosting, email, video, payments, analytics, advertising), under obligations to protect it;
  • with the teacher assigned to your session, limited to what they need to teach you;
  • when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, our teachers or the public;
  • in connection with a merger, sale or transfer of our business, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

7. Data retention

We keep booking and correspondence records for as long as needed to provide our services and meet tax, accounting and legal requirements, generally no longer than three years after your last session. You can ask us to delete your information sooner, subject to those requirements.

8. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live, including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other US states with consumer privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we have collected about you and how it is used;
  • access, correct or delete your personal information;
  • opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising;
  • not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To make a request, email hello@asanaatlas.us with the subject line “Privacy request.” We will verify your request and respond within the time required by law. You may use an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as an opt-out of targeted advertising where required.

9. Children’s privacy

The Site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we do not offer sessions to anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian’s consent and presence. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Links to other websites

The Site links to external websites we don’t control. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date above, and for significant changes we will provide a more prominent notice.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: hello@asanaatlas.us.
